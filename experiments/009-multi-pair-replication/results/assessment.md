# Assessment

- Experiment 009 completed 36 generations at the frozen model revision and settings. Two sorting pairs passed all six endpoint controls; the non-sorting byte-histogram pair was blocked by its B endpoint as required by the specification.
- For the two endpoint-valid sorting pairs, per-step dual-prompt logit mixing produced zero hybrids in six midpoint runs, and final-state hard projection produced zero hybrids in six midpoint runs.
- Direct DiffusionGemma prompting produced both required parent concepts in all six runs across the two valid pairs. One fast-path/fallback output contained a damaged token near `heapq.heappop`, but its two-path strategy remained identifiable.
- In `pair-vectorized-counting`, all three dual-logit midpoints retained bounded counting sort but lost NumPy, `bincount`, and `repeat`. All three hard midpoints abandoned both parents and proposed prohibited built-in sorting. Direct prompting combined the techniques in 3/3 runs.
- In `pair-fast-path-fallback`, one dual-logit midpoint retained the NumPy fast path alone and two were malformed without either complete parent. All three hard midpoints abandoned both parents and proposed prohibited built-in sorting. Direct prompting combined the conditional NumPy fast path and heapq fallback in 3/3 runs.
- The two hard midpoint initial canvases were already incoherent token fragments. Denoising sharpened them into unrelated built-in sorting rather than recovering either intended combination.
- Across midpoint steps, source top-1 agreement averaged 0.790 for vectorized counting and 0.747 for fast-path/fallback. Mixed top-1 tokens matched neither source at only 0.058 and 0.092 respectively, yet the outputs still did not coordinate both multi-token concepts.
- The non-sorting NumPy endpoint passed 3/3. The multiprocessing endpoint passed only 1/3 under the predefined lexical gate; all three outputs shifted from independent chunk processing and summing partial histograms toward workers updating shared memory, and two omitted the required chunk concept. Its midpoint and direct-prompt runs were correctly skipped.
- Therefore the sorting result replicates across two additional pair structures: the tested numerical midpoint methods produced 0/12 hybrids while direct prompting produced 6/6. The experiment does not establish cross-workload replication because the non-sorting endpoint control failed.
- The next step must retain the frozen byte-histogram workload and diagnose or strengthen only its multiprocessing endpoint before testing its midpoint. It must not replace the pair based on this result.
- Scope: DiffusionGemma revision `f7f5b7f5fa82ffc52addd066915886d497f5517b`, repository revision `67385d7`, three seeds, eight denoising steps, 96-token canvases, two endpoint-valid sorting pairs, and one blocked non-sorting pair.
